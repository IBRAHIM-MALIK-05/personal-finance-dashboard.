How the numbers work

Actual, forecast and plan

Every number carries a label with a shape, so you never have to rely on colour:

  • Actual (filled square): calculated only from transactions that have happened.
  • Forecast (dashed square): an estimate about the future. It can be wrong.
  • Actual + forecast: part history, part estimate (for example a month-end budget forecast).
  • Your plan (circle): a number you chose, such as a savings plan or a what-if change.

Select “How is this worked out?” under any number to see the formula, every input and the assumptions.

The forecast

Balance today, plus recurring income and bills that have not happened yet, minus typical everyday spending. Typical spending is the median of your last three full months for each category. The shaded range shows what happens if you spend like your lowest or highest month. A recurring bill counts as paid only when a transaction is linked to it. A payment that just looks similar is shown as “may already be paid” until you confirm it.

Limits of this prototype

  • All data is synthetic. It is not connected to any bank, and it never asks for bank credentials.
  • Demo profiles are not real logins. The coach’s access is simulated, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • This is a learning prototype. It does not give financial or investment advice.
